## Rules that always apply
- One metaphor per beat; motion until the last frame.
- Drawn SVG/CSS only, no emoji, no AI images for text or UI.
- Real numbers only: if no real figure exists, show the visual without one.
- Replicas of real apps look like the real app (window chrome, real labels).
- Content inside y 480-1350 of 1080x1920.
